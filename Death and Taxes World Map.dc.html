How to read this plate

  • The continent is enemy territory. Vorgathul is the only ground the Zug'thar hold, and it is roughly two percent of the landmass — every raid is an incursion, and every extraction is a run back to a single point.
  • Ashreach is the pressure valve. Humans will not settle the burn ring, so it is where orc raiding parties form up and where returning players are most often caught — the natural PvP band.
  • Difficulty runs outward from the volcano, but not evenly — Bellhollow and Saltbrace are the richest and the furthest, which puts the best loot at the end of the longest way home.
  • Province borders are indicative. The coastline, the offshore islands and the internal boundaries are all Lane A's to author against terrain.

Open on the map

OpenWhether Vaelmoor is one kingdom or several provinces with their own agendas — the master doc holds this open, and this plate assumes a single crown with provincial holdings.
